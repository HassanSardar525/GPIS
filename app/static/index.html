    What the prototype measures

    Education

    Average of total literacy, female literacy and gender parity. Census 2023.

    Health

    Relative index of listed public health facilities per 100,000 people within these ten districts. It does not measure care quality.

    Accountability

    Average overall and development-budget utilization. Spending money is not proof that it was spent well.
